FAQ

Before weget started.

01

Do you write the copy or do I need to provide it?

We can handle it both ways. You can provide your own content, or our copywriters can craft professional, SEO-optimized copy tailored specifically to your brand and goals.

02

Can I see the design before you build it?

Absolutely. We don't start the development phase until you have reviewed and fully approved the high-fidelity design mockups in Figma.

03

What happens after launch?

We don’t just disappear. We provide post-launch technical support, training for your team on how to manage the site, and options for ongoing maintenance and scaling.

04

What if I'm not happy with the result?

We work in clear milestones (wireframes, design, development). By requiring your approval at each stage, we ensure the final product aligns perfectly with your vision before it’s even finished.
